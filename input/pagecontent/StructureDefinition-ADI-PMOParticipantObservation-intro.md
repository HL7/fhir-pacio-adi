The ADI PMO Participant Observation profile identifies individuals who participated in establishing a Portable Medical Order (PMO), including family members or other persons with a personal or legal relationship to the patient who supported or agreed with the decisions reflected in the PMO, as well as involved professionals. Capturing participants may be particularly important when the patient has diminished decision-making capacity and a legally authorized representative, such as a guardian, participates in healthcare decision-making on the patient’s behalf.
